\section{Quarto layout issue involving long tables}

\subsection{Issue}\label{issue}

When a floating graphic is placed on the next page, the presence of a
table on that next page will cause the text to continue beyond the
bottom margin. The issue does not happen if instead of the table, there
is another figure.

\subsection{To reproduce the issue}\label{reproissue}

\begin{enumerate}
\item
  Create a text. Insert a Figure between certain paragraphs, and insert
  a table a few paragraphs later. After the table, insert plenty of more
  paragraphs.
\item
  Render the pdf.
\item
  Insert paragraphs before the Figure such that the remaining space on
  the same page is not enough
\item
  Render the pdf.
\end{enumerate}

\textbf{Expected result:} Some paragraphs after the Figure are continued
on the same page until the bottom margin. The figure is moved to the
other page. \emph{Text after the table correctly flows to to the
subsequent space.}

\textbf{Observed result:} \emph{Text after the table continues onto the
bottom margin of the page}

\section{Demonstration}\label{demonstration}

After the next paragraph, we insert a figure. After that, we insert
two other paragraph and a simple table. Render the document and see the
layout. Then uncomment the paragraph below, and render again to see the
issue.

Deleting this paragraph will free enough room for the figure on the
current page to fit the figure. It will then remain on the current page,
and there will be no issue with the text and table that follows after.
