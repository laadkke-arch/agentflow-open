A hacky solution, but one that should also work with the oldish pandoc 2.9, is to insert the horizontal line with a raw LaTeX command in the table. It works, but the results aren't as pleasant, because the line isn't vertically centered between the rows.

|  ABC  |  DEF  |  GHI  |  JKL  |
|:-----:|:-----:|:-----:|:-----:|
|  1    |  123  | 456   |  789  |
|  2    |  234  | 567   |  890  |
|  3    |  345  | 678   |  901  |
|`\hline`{=tex} |  111  | 222   |  333  |
  : Foo bar baz quux, mumble blurf.
