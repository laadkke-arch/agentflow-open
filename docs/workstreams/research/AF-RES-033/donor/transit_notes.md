# Transit donor collection notes

## Independence and finite sampling frame

This collector read only repository/workflow instructions, provider-scope lines 32–47, the shared capture helper, and its own transit search/capture material. No recipient inputs, frame, questions, rankings, or other research outputs were read. Search titles and snippets did expose donor text; that exposure is recorded honestly.

Six official original-English works are selected in the sealed issuer order: King County Metro, MBTA, TTC, TransLink, TriMet and SFMTA. Neither fallback was needed. There is exactly one selected original per issuer. The first initial query for each issuer used the exact sealed Boolean field query. The 22 returned initial candidates were frozen and sorted by Unicode-normalized lowercase full title and URL before full-body opening. Official linked-directory stages followed initial failures. Directory results are finite returned pages, not site censuses.

The public candidate manifest retains every returned title and URL, including duplicate representations and later unselected items, so ordering remains reviewable. Some broad SFMTA directory queries returned noisy first pages and pagination links; those lists remain in the manifest. An observed Service Standards category then yielded a finite page with 63 report/document links. The first otherwise eligible work in that ordered page was the SRTP family. Mandatory same-work version verification used the exact quoted family phrase across all official report categories and returned 14 report pages / 46 linked representations; 2022 was the latest edition found in that frame.

## Selection and version decisions

MBTA's empty initial search triggered its official policy directory. A parser expecting heading elements initially found no cards; the DOM used title divs, which were corrected and frozen before any linked candidate body was opened. The Diversion Service Standards landing and PDF are one work.

TTC's initial meeting, review-engagement and planning-directory pages did not issue a current operating standard. Its directory links the May 2024 original. TransLink's topic hubs described and linked the operational guideline and report history; the two network-management addresses have identical extracted bodies and count once as an excluded hub.

TriMet's initial 2025 PDF addresses failed with HTTP 503 and 404. The observed official parent-directory redirect led to its current civil-rights page and available 2025 PDF. The report retains a draft note even though the policy part describes Board-approved rules; final adoption of the whole compiled report is not asserted.

The root resolved a provider-only field boundary before any recipient read: an eligible document must prescribe explicit operating-service change (timetable/frequency/span, route-service configuration, or suspension/change procedure). Stop-location-only or construction-clearance rules do not suffice. The SFMTA Blue Book was excluded on that factual condition, and the frozen title order continued. The 2017 SRTP met the operating field, but subsequent mandatory same-work verification found newer 2019 and 2022 editions. The selected 2022 narrative includes explicit recurring service-change procedures; its official landing is dated December 27, 2022. Its latest status is confined to that frozen directory-family result frame. The older editions are not additional originals in the minimum count.

## Text boundaries and rights

Raw originals and full extracted text exist only under gitignored donor/cache/TRANSIT-* paths. Public JSONL contains metadata, hashes, provenance, neutral summaries and limitations; it contains zero verbatim body-excerpt words. Bibliographic titles and exact section identifiers are retained as audit metadata. No open license was assumed.

Long manuals use entire named operational chapters/parts, with deterministic contiguous physical-PDF page boundaries. Short policies/narratives retain their complete operational bodies. All subsections, tables, exceptions and notes within those boundaries are retained in original order. Cover titles, navigation and running headers/footers are omitted. No sentences were selected for their downstream usefulness. Extracted bodies are UTF-8 with canonical LF, preserved line structure/interior spacing, and separate raw and body SHA-256 values. Tables and columns are represented by Poppler layout text, with layout limitations recorded. SFMTA's separately linked data spreadsheet is not inside the narrative PDF and is not claimed as included.

The source registry also records unique substantively opened excluded candidates. Each has counted_minimum=false and a precise read-scope note; it does not imply every excluded manual was read word-for-word. Purely navigational visits, title-only exclusions and failed fetches remain in attempts. The SFMTA 2017 full-text cache is a superseded edition of the selected work, not an additional minimum document.
