<div id="introduction" class="section level1">
<h1><span class="header-section-number">1</span> Introduction</h1>
<p>This document gives an introduction to and overview of the quality control functionality of the <em><a href="https://bioconductor.org/packages/3.11/scater">scater</a></em> package.
<em><a href="https://bioconductor.org/packages/3.11/scater">scater</a></em> contains tools to help with the analysis of single-cell transcriptomic data,
focusing on low-level steps such as quality control, normalization and visualization.
It is based on the <code>SingleCellExperiment</code> class (from the <em><a href="https://bioconductor.org/packages/3.11/SingleCellExperiment">SingleCellExperiment</a></em> package),
and thus is interoperable with many other Bioconductor packages such as <em><a href="https://bioconductor.org/packages/3.11/scran">scran</a></em>, <em><a href="https://bioconductor.org/packages/3.11/batchelor">batchelor</a></em> and <em><a href="https://bioconductor.org/packages/3.11/iSEE">iSEE</a></em>.</p>
<p><strong>Note:</strong> A more comprehensive description of the use of <em><a href="https://bioconductor.org/packages/3.11/scater">scater</a></em> (along with other packages) in a scRNA-seq analysis workflow is available at <a href="https://osca.bioconductor.org" class="uri">https://osca.bioconductor.org</a>.</p>
